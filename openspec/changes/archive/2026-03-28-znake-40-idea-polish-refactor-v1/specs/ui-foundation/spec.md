## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: UI controls preserve readability and interaction clarity under pressure

The UI foundation SHALL provide minimum legibility and focus/target affordances for run and menu interactions.

#### Scenario: Focus and touch affordances are visible
- **WHEN** interactive controls are rendered in menu/run overlays
- **THEN** focus-visible state SHALL be clearly visible
- **AND** touch-oriented controls SHALL meet a minimum target size floor.

#### Scenario: Reduced-motion preference minimizes non-essential motion
- **WHEN** reduced motion accessibility state is enabled
- **THEN** non-essential transitions and decorative motion SHALL be reduced or disabled.
